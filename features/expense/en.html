How to split travel expenses

No more collecting receipts and building a spreadsheet after you get home. Record each expense as it happens, and the app takes care of the settlement.

  1. Create a trip and invite friends

    Invited trip mates see the same expense history and can add to it. You choose separately for each friend whether they can edit expenses.

  2. Record what you spend

    Tap Add Expense and enter the amount, currency, category and payment method (cash or card). Pick a trip day such as DAY 1 or DAY 2, or record costs paid in advance, like flights and hotels, under Preparation.

  3. Choose who paid and who owes

    Select Paid by and Owed by, then tap the 1/N button to divide the amount evenly. If people ordered different things, enter each person's amount yourself.

  4. Check the Settlement tab

    Somewhere compares what each person paid with what they spent, and shows how much to send to or receive from whom. Make those transfers and you're settled.

Travel abroad without currency math

Record expenses in the local currency, whether that's yen in Japan or euros in Europe. Somewhere supports 50 currencies, and each trip can use up to 3 of them at once.

Amounts are converted with automatically loaded exchange rates, and the trip host can also enter the rate you actually got. Totals and settlement amounts can be shown in any of the trip's currencies. The Rates & Currencies screen also includes an exchange rate calculator.

Made for trips like these

  • Trips with a shared fund

    Contributions, expenses paid from the fund and the remaining balance all count toward the settlement

  • Taking turns to pay

    You pay today, a friend pays tomorrow, and it all evens out in one settlement at the end

  • Trips abroad

    Add your home currency to the trip to see totals and settlement in it, even when you record in the local currency

  • Expenses just for you

    Expenses marked Private are hidden from trip mates and left out of the settlement

See where your money went

The Statistics tab shows how much you spent in each category: Sightseeing, Museums, Activities, Shopping, Food, Transport, Flights, Lodging and Other. You can also see how your spending splits between cash and card.

The History tab groups expenses by trip day, and Trip Stats in your profile shows your total travel expenses and daily average across all your trips.

Frequently asked questions

Is expense splitting free?

Recording expenses, currency conversion, shared funds, statistics and settlement are all free. Attaching photos such as receipts to expenses is available with Somewhere Pro.

Do my friends need the app to split costs?

Yes. To be part of the settlement, friends need to join the trip in Somewhere by invitation. You can invite friends for free, and Somewhere Pro lets you invite more.

Which currencies are supported?

Somewhere supports 50 currencies, including the US dollar, euro, British pound, Japanese yen, Korean won and Thai baht. Each trip can use up to 3 currencies at once.

How are exchange rates set?

Somewhere converts amounts with automatically loaded exchange rates by default, and the trip host can also enter rates manually. Automatic rates may differ from the rate you actually got, so treat them as a reference.
