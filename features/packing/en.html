How to pack with SOMEWHERE

No more passing a notes-app list back and forth with friends. Tap the packing button on a trip to open a checklist for that trip.

  1. Add common essentials at once

    Tap “Add essentials” to see things people often pack, like a passport, phone, power bank and medicine. Keep only what you need and add them in one go. Items already on your list are not added twice.

  2. Add anything else you need

    Type an item in the box at the bottom and it is added right away. You can also group items, such as a charger and a travel adapter under “Electronics”.

  3. Keep it tidy

    Press and hold an item, then drag it to change the order. Add a divider to split the items around it into separate cards, so clothes, toiletries and documents are easy to tell apart.

  4. Check off what you packed

    Check items as they go into your bag. The packing button on the trip shows how many items you have packed so far.

Shared and private lists

Packing comes in two lists: shared and private. Everyone on the trip sees the shared list, while only you can see your private list.

  • Shared list

    Things only one person needs to bring, like a power strip, medicine or snacks, so everyone can see when they are packed

  • Private list

    Your own things, like a passport, clothes and toiletries, kept on a list only you can see

Reuse lists from past trips

Copy one item or a whole list and paste it into the packing list of another trip. When you duplicate a trip to plan a similar one, you can copy its packing lists too.

Frequently asked questions

Can my trip mates see my private packing list in SOMEWHERE?

No, only you can see your private packing list in the SOMEWHERE travel planner app. Trip mates see the shared packing list only.

Who can edit the shared packing list in SOMEWHERE?

Trip mates who can edit the itinerary in the SOMEWHERE travel planner app can also add and check items on the shared packing list. Every trip mate can edit their own private packing list.

Can I duplicate past trips and packing lists in SOMEWHERE?

Yes. When you duplicate a trip in the SOMEWHERE travel planner app to plan a similar one, you can copy its shared and private packing lists too, or copy and paste individual items between trips.

Can I view a packing list offline in SOMEWHERE?

Yes, a packing list you have opened before in the SOMEWHERE travel planner app can be viewed offline. Adding or checking items needs an internet connection.
