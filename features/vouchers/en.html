all in your trip

Gather the confirmations scattered across your inbox and photos into each trip in the SOMEWHERE travel planner app. Add a photo or PDF and AI fills in the booking code, dates and times, ready to show at the airport or check-in desk.

Free on the App Store and Google Play

How to keep your bookings

Tap the vouchers and tickets button on a trip to see all of its bookings. Switch between the card view, where you can press and hold to reorder tickets, and the day-by-day view sorted by time within each day.

  1. Pick a ticket type

    Add flights, trains, buses and ferries as transport, hotels and other stays as a stay, and entry tickets, tours and passes as general. Each type shows only the fields it needs.

  2. Fill it in from a photo or PDF

    Choose “Fill with AI” and add a photo or PDF of your confirmation. AI finds details such as the booking code, dates and times, and where you depart and arrive. You can also type it in yourself, and check or correct anything AI filled in before you save.

  3. Keep the original files

    Attach photos and PDF files of the confirmation to each ticket. PDFs open in the app, where you can scroll through pages and zoom in.

  4. Pull it up when you need it

    A flight shows like a boarding pass, with departure and arrival, times, flight number and seat at a glance. Copy the booking code or reservation name with one tap.

Link them to your itinerary and expenses

Link a ticket to a place in your itinerary to open its photos and PDFs right from that place or from the current trip card on your trips list. Your flight ticket is on the airport stop and your hotel voucher is on the hotel stop, without switching screens.

Link the expense you paid for a flight or stay under the ticket’s payments to see what it cost in the same place, and press and hold any linked item to unlink it anytime

Frequently asked questions

What travel bookings can I save in SOMEWHERE?

In the SOMEWHERE travel planner app, you can save transport tickets such as flights, trains, buses and ferries, and hotel or other stay vouchers. General tickets such as museum entry, tours and passes work too.

How does filling vouchers with AI work in SOMEWHERE?

Filling with AI in the SOMEWHERE travel planner app uses credits, and when you are short on credits you can watch an ad to use it. Information filled in by AI may contain mistakes, so check it before you save.

Can I open travel vouchers offline and keep some private in SOMEWHERE?

Yes. Ticket and voucher details you have loaded once in the SOMEWHERE travel planner app can be opened offline, and SOMEWHERE PRO lets you add and edit private vouchers visible only to you.

What does SOMEWHERE PRO add for vouchers?

With SOMEWHERE PRO in the SOMEWHERE travel planner app, you can save more vouchers and tickets, and add or edit private vouchers and tickets that only you can see

Are tickets copied when I duplicate a trip in SOMEWHERE?

No, bookings belong to the original trip in the SOMEWHERE travel planner app, so vouchers and tickets are not copied when you duplicate it
