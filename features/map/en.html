How to plan your route on a map

A plain list of places doesn't tell you which order makes sense. Somewhere shows your route on the map while you build the itinerary.

  1. Add places to each day

    Tap Add item on a day and find a spot with Search places, or choose From map to pick a location right on the map. Each place can have a category, a time and a memo.

  2. Add travel between places

    Pick how you're getting there on a Travel item, such as walking, bus, subway, taxi, train or plane, to see the straight-line distance and an estimated travel time. If you enter a start and end time, the scheduled duration is shown instead.

  3. Check your route on the map

    Tap Map on the trip screen to see every place numbered in visiting order and connected by a line in each day's color. Show only the days you want, or filter by category.

  4. Reorder and move places

    In edit mode, drag places to reorder them, or use Move to another date to put a place on a different day. The new order shows up in the numbers and lines on the map.

Import places from Google Maps

In Google Maps, tap Share on a place and choose Somewhere to save it to Saved items in the trip you choose. Then use Add to itinerary to put saved places on any day.

Get directions with Google Maps on the go

Tap Open in Google Maps on a place to jump straight to it in Google Maps. On a Travel item, tap Google Maps directions to open the route between the places before and after it, using the travel mode you set. Tap My location on the map to see where you are.

Frequently asked questions

Is the map free?

The trip map, place search, My location and Google Maps links are all free. Somewhere Pro lets you keep more places in Saved items.

Do the route lines follow real roads?

No. The lines are straight lines that connect places in itinerary order. For turn-by-turn routes, tap Google Maps directions to open them in Google Maps.

Does it optimize my route automatically?

Not automatically. Drag places into a new order yourself, or ask the AI itinerary editor to optimize your route.

Which map does Somewhere use?

Somewhere is built on Google Maps, so you can search for and view places anywhere in the world
