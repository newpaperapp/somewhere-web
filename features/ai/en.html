How to plan a trip with AI

Know where you're going but not where to start? Let AI get you started. Answer four questions and you'll get an itinerary you can edit right away.

  1. Choose Plan with AI

    When you create a new trip, tap Plan with AI instead of Plan manually. Then type the city or country you're visiting.

  2. Set dates and companions

    Pick your travel dates, then choose who you're traveling with: Solo, Partner, Friends or Family

  3. Choose your trip type

    Pick as many as you like from Cultural experiences, Historical sites, Exhibitions & Museums, Sports & Activity, Shopping, Relaxation & Healing, and Nature & Scenery

  4. AI builds your days

    AI picks well-known places at your destination and fills each day with stops, times and memos. Meals and lodging are included too, and sights are pinned on the map.

Edit your itinerary by chatting with AI

In edit mode on the trip screen, tap Edit with AI to open the AI itinerary editor. Ask things like “Recommend a cozy cafe for the afternoon,” “Switch to indoor spots for a rainy day” or “Optimize the route to save travel time,” and it adds places or changes the order and times based on your current itinerary. AI changes show up in edit mode first and are only kept when you tap Save.

Keep planning like any other trip

An AI-made trip works just like one you built yourself: add places, reorder them, invite friends and record expenses. You can also open the AI editor from a place's screen or from Saved items to edit or find places. AI writes your itinerary in your app language.

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI trip planner free?

AI features use credits, which you can earn for free by watching ads. Somewhere Pro also includes credits every week.

Can I trust an AI-made itinerary as is?

Generated trips may contain incorrect information. Double-check opening hours and travel times before you go.

Do I need to sign in?

Yes, AI features work once you're signed in and connected to the internet

Are my expenses sent to AI?

No. Expenses, photos and trip mate information are never sent to AI. AI uses your itinerary's titles, place locations and memos plus your conversation.
